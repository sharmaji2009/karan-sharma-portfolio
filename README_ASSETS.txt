PORTFOLIO ASSETS
================

1. PROFILE PHOTO
Rename your full/portrait photo to:
profile.jpg

Put it here:
public/profile.jpg

Recommended: a clear vertical photo, ideally 1000px+ wide.

2. RESUME
Rename your resume PDF to:
resume.pdf

Put it here:
public/resume.pdf

3. SOCIAL LINKS
Open:
src/main.jsx

Find the GitHub, LinkedIn and Instagram links inside the Contact section
and replace them with your real profile URLs.

4. EMAIL
Your current portfolio email is:
sharmaji9742@gmail.com

To change it, edit the EMAIL constant at the top of src/main.jsx.

5. DIRECT CONTACT
The contact form uses a mailto flow. When a visitor submits it,
their default email application opens with the recipient, subject and
message pre-filled. No backend is required for this version.
